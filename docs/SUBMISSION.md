# Submission copy and evidence boundary

## Judge-facing copy

**Zero Infinity is a decision operating surface that makes evidence, opposition, and authority visible before action.** Independent reasoning roles produce inspectable artifacts; a council records the decision and its provenance. Paper and shadow modes are bounded and no-write. Live is locked until the missing operational and authority gates exist.

## Evidence ceiling

The local implementation proves deterministic reasoning, receipt/provenance structure, readiness reporting, and no-write paper/shadow behavior. It does not prove hosted REST/MCP availability, exchange connectivity, testnet execution, live authority, or economic performance. The static Pages artifact proves presentation only.

## 90–120 second video script

- **0:00–0:12** — Open the surface: “This is the decision trace, not a trading dashboard. The authority posture is explicit.”
- **0:12–0:30** — Walk the pipeline from Evidence through Opposition and Council to ReasoningReceipt. Point out the current TradeThesis and the pending gates.
- **0:30–0:48** — Open Reasoning Stacks. Show Advocate, Opposer, Market Analyst, and Council composition and the provenance/hash boundary.
- **0:48–1:05** — Open Proof / Readiness. Show reasoning available, paper no-write, hosted evidence absent, testnet blocked, and live locked.
- **1:05–1:20** — Toggle the explicitly labeled local demo fixture. Explain that it is presentation data, not a receipt or hosted proof.
- **1:20–1:40** — Show local REST `/health` and local MCP `get_readiness` commands. Explain both share the same service and have no live write endpoint.
- **1:40–1:55** — Close with the honest boundary: Pages hosts the UI only; a server host and further evidence are required for hosted operation.
